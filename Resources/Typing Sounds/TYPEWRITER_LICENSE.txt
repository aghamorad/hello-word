Typewriter sound source

"WWS Typewriter.ogg" records an Erika 5 mechanical typewriter.
Sound recordist: Konrad Gutkowski
Source collection: Work With Sounds
Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWS_Typewriter.ogg

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Hello, Word uses short, edited excerpts from the recording for its Typewriter
key, space, and delete actions. The excerpts were converted to mono 22.05 kHz
PCM, trimmed, filtered, faded, and level-adjusted. The original recording
included a line-end bell, but Hello, Word deliberately does not use it.

The carriage-return action is an edited excerpt from "Typewriter Carriage
Return.wav" by ramsamba:
https://freesound.org/people/ramsamba/sounds/318686/

That recording was released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The sound recordist and Work With Sounds do not endorse Hello, Word.
